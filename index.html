Good to know

Questions we get a lot

Do I need to book, or can I walk in?
Walk-ins are welcome. That said, a quick call or WhatsApp to 0755 406 162 means your seat is ready when you arrive — worth doing on weekends and at month-end.
Can you do a design I saw on Instagram or TikTok?
Usually, yes. Send the picture on WhatsApp before you come and we'll confirm what's possible, what it will cost and roughly how long it takes.
How long does a full set take?
About an hour for gel, and an hour and a half to two hours for tips, builder gel or detailed art. Removing an old set adds a little time on top.
Do you remove nails done somewhere else?
Yes — removal is KES 100 per hand, done gently so your natural nail isn't damaged in the process.
How long will my nails last?
With proper prep and normal wear, a good set holds two to three weeks. Come back for a refill rather than picking them off — your natural nail will thank you.
How do I pay?
M-Pesa or cash, whichever is easier for you.
